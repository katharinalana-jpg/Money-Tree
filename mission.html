Why we exist

At Portemonnaie, it is our mission to fight:

25%

EU gender pension gap

Women aged 65 and older receive one quarter less in retirement income than men.

29%

Less invested monthly

European women invest 29% less of their monthly income than men on average.

€100k

Lifetime wealth deficit

Women accumulate an estimated €100,000 less in wealth over their lifetime.

Portemonnaie exists to close the gender wealth gap. Because every investment is a vote. We build a platform that lets you align your values, makes investment easy, and is designed from the ground up so everyone can make their money speak and secure their future.

A new definition of growth

A new era of investing.

At Portemonnaie, we believe that growth must be measured across four dimensions. Every company relies on financial capital, environmental capital, social capital, and network capital. For decades, only the growth of financial capital has counted as success. We believe that must change.
