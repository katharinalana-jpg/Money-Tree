The core experience

Start investing according to your values in only five steps.

The Money Tree Robo Advisor guides you from your very first steps to your real portfolio.

  1. 01

    Take the quiz

    Answer a few questions about your investing style, your goals, and the causes you care about.

  2. 02

    Find your archetype

    Get a clear overview and a feeling for what your values aligned portfolio can look like.

  3. 03

    Explore

    Browse ETFs and stocks ranked by a sustainability score and a gender score to make impact investing and gender lens investing easy.

  4. 04

    Fill your basket

    Drag and drop to build your portfolio. Investing will feel like online shopping.

  5. 05

    Execute

    Open your first depot or let a trusted wealth manager do it for you.

Why we exist

At Money Tree, it is our mission to fight:

25%

EU gender pension gap

Women aged 65 and older receive one quarter less in retirement income than men.

29%

Less invested monthly

European women invest 29% less of their monthly income than men on average.

€100k

Lifetime wealth deficit

Women accumulate an estimated €100,000 less in wealth over their lifetime.

These gaps do not close on their own. They close when women are enabled to confidently take control of their finances and invest in what they believe in.

Money Tree exists to close the financial gender gap. Because every investment is a vote. We build a platform that lets you align your values, makes investment easy, and is designed from the ground up so everyone can make their money speak and secure their future.”

A new definition of growth

A new era of investing.

At Money Tree, we believe that growth must be measured across four dimensions. Every company relies on financial capital, environmental capital, social capital, and network capital. For decades, only the growth of financial capital has counted as success. We believe that must change.
