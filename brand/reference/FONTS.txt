Portemonnaie Finance Brand Fonts

All three fonts are free and open source on Google Fonts.
Download the families below, then install all weights on your system,
on every device that produces brand material, and on every collaborator
machine.

1. INTER  (Sans-serif, primary body and UI font)
   https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter
   Weights to install: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold,
                       800 ExtraBold, 900 Black

2. INSTRUMENT SERIF  (Serif italic, accent and quotation font)
   https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif
   Weights to install: 400 Regular, 400 Italic

3. CAVEAT  (Handwritten, signature and brand-phrase font)
   https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Caveat
   Weights to install: 500 Medium, 700 Bold

For web (HTML, landing pages, app, deck):
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700;800;900&family=Instrument+Serif:ital@0;1&family=Caveat:wght@500;700&display=swap');

For PowerPoint, Word, Keynote, Figma, Canva:
Install the fonts on your computer first. Then they appear in the font picker
of every application.
